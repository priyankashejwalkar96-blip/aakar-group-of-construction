Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or submit a quote or contact form, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and city
  • Project details such as service type, budget range and start timeline
  • Technical data such as browser type, device and pages visited

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide quotes
  • To schedule consultations and site visits
  • To improve our website, services and customer experience
  • To send project updates you have asked for

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business, or when required by law.

4. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar tools to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
