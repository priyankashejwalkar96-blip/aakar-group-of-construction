Crafting Timeless Landmarks

Crafting high-quality spaces that reflect excellence and lasting value

Aakar Group of Constructions is a leading construction company in Nashik, Maharashtra, offering expert services in residential construction, commercial building projects, and project management consultancy across India. Our journey began in 2017 with a simple belief—to build spaces that people can truly call their own. From our roots in rural Nashik to delivering 150+ successful projects, every structure we create reflects dedication, care, and responsibility. We work closely with our clients and architects to turn their visions into reality.

With a strong focus on structural integrity, RCC quality, and material standards, we ensure that every stage of construction—from foundation and reinforcement to finishing and handover—is executed with precision and accountability.
