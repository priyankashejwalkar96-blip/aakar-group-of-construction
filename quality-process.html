Construction Quality Checklist

We follow a detailed checklist at every stage to ensure accuracy, safety, and long-term durability.

01

Centerline Marking Checklist

Layout Accuracy

KEY CHECKS

  • Layout marking as approved drawings
  • Proper alignment and right-angle verification
  • Diagonal measurement accuracy
  • Benchmark level confirmation
  • Grid lines clearly marked on site
PURPOSE

Ensures the entire structure layout is accurate from the beginning

02

Excavation Checklist

Foundation Groundwork

KEY CHECKS

  • Excavation depth as per drawing
  • Width and alignment of footing
  • Soil condition and bearing capacity
  • Removal of loose soil and debris
  • Proper slope and safety measures
  • Water accumulation check and dewatering
PURPOSE

Provides a strong and stable base for foundation

03

Ground Beam Checklist

Structural Foundation

REINFORCEMENT CHECKS

  • Steel diameter and spacing as per design
  • Proper binding and placement
  • Cover blocks provided correctly
  • Lap length maintained

FORMWORK CHECKS

  • Alignment and level of shuttering
  • Proper support and tight joints

CONCRETING CHECKS

  • Concrete grade verification
  • Proper vibration during pouring
  • Level finishing · Curing process initiated
PURPOSE

Ensures structural strength and load distribution

04

Column Checklist

Vertical Load Members

STEEL REINFORCEMENT

  • Vertical bar size and number
  • Ring (stirrups) size and spacing
  • Proper lapping of bars
  • Alignment and verticality

CONCRETING

  • Column size as per drawing
  • Proper vibration during filling
  • No honeycombing or voids
  • Surface finishing quality
PURPOSE

Maintains structural integrity and load-bearing capacity

05

General Site Quality Checks

Ongoing Monitoring

ONGOING CHECKS

  • Material quality verification
  • Proper storage of cement, steel, and sand
  • Regular site supervision
  • Safety measures implementation
  • Work execution as per drawings
06

Documentation & Reporting

Accountability Trail

RECORDS MAINTAINED

  • Checklist maintained for every stage
  • Site inspection records updated
  • Engineer approvals at each stage
  • Issue tracking and correction

Materials & RCC Standards

Certified materials and detailed quality checklists keep every project consistent and safe.
